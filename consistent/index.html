Consistent support

One thing. 1,000 repetitions. One more.

Consistent helps you focus on one journey and make every repetition visible.

Getting started

Name your journey, define what counts as one repetition, and begin. Already started? Add a starting point between 0 and 999.

Correcting a repetition

Use Undo immediately after logging, or open Settings → Repetition History to remove an accidental entry.

Keeping your progress

Pausing and archiving preserve your work. Your journeys are stored on your device; Consistent does not provide cloud synchronization.
