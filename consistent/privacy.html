CONSISTENT

Privacy policy

Updated October 11, 2026

Consistent is provided by KeletaMedia LLC.

Your progress stays on your device

Consistent stores your journeys, repetition history, and preferences locally on your device. The app does not transmit that information to KeletaMedia LLC.

No account is required. Consistent does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your journey data.

Your choices

You can correct repetitions and delete journeys within the app. Deleting a journey removes its associated repetition history. Device backups are managed by iOS and your Apple settings.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending information you do not want to share.

For support or privacy questions, email support@keletamedia.com.
